# Bundled fonts

## Liberation Fonts 2.1.5

Liberation Serif, with regular, bold, italic and bold italic faces, provides a Times New Roman-style reading font. Sans-serif uses the Android system font and requires no bundled font files.

Upstream: https://github.com/liberationfonts/liberation-fonts/releases/tag/2.1.5

Unmodified binaries from the upstream TTF release. Licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Full copyright and license: [Liberation-OFL.txt](app/src/main/assets/licenses/Liberation-OFL.txt).

## Courier Prime

Courier-style monospaced font, with regular, bold, italic and bold italic faces.

Upstream: https://github.com/quoteunquoteapps/CourierPrime
Google Fonts snapshot: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/986a8089ce76db1064d1baf745dcde8cde756f45/ofl/courierprime

Unmodified binaries licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Full copyright and license: [CourierPrime-OFL.txt](app/src/main/assets/licenses/CourierPrime-OFL.txt).

These are open font alternatives, not the proprietary Microsoft fonts.
